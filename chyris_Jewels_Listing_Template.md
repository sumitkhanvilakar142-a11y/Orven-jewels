```

```
[Product Type] [Material] [Style] [Gender] - [Brand]

## 6. COMPLIANCE / DISCLOSURE NOTES (IMPORTANT)

- **925 Silver**: Since it's real silver, purity claim "925" should be accurate; consider mentioning if BIS hallmarked or not.
- **916 Gold**: If your product is **gold-plated** (not solid 22K gold), you MUST clearly say "Gold Plated" in the title and description — writing just "916 Gold" without "plated" for a plated item is misleading and can get your listing flagged/removed on Amazon & Meesho, and violates Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) rules in India.
- If it IS solid 22K/916 hallmarked gold, BIS hallmark number (HUID) must be mentioned as required by Indian law for gold jewelry above prescribed value.
